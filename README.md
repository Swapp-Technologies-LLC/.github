### Welcome to Swapp Technologies LLC 👋

## 🌐 The Swapp Technologies Open-Source Mission

Swapp Technologies LLC is committed to building practical, developer-first software designed to eliminate day-to-day workflow friction and reduce operational overhead. Our open-source journey is rooted in years of shipping custom integrations, architecting scalable digital platforms, and learning from the incredible global community of developers.

Having faced the manual bottlenecks, spiraling hosting costs, and complexity of modern deployment pipelines firsthand, we design tools that keep things simple, secure, and cost-effective. We believe in providing robust, production-ready utilities that empower creators, small agencies, and developers to build high-performance digital experiences without expensive database dependencies.

## 🤝 Our Commitment to a Sustainable Ecosystem

We don't just stand on the shoulders of open-source giants; we actively support them. To help build a sustainable software ecosystem:

Community-First: All our public repositories are built with MIT-standard open access, inviting collaboration, forks, and community improvements.
Giving Back: We dedicate 20% of all sponsorships and Open Collective funding received across our portfolio directly back to the upstream libraries, runtime engines, and core dependencies (such as the Ghost Foundation, Electron, Pagefind, and others) that make our software possible.
Whether it’s static site automation, local containerized workflows, or client-side utilities, Swapp Technologies is dedicated to shipping clean, secure code that solves real problems.
